# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or beliefs.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, kind, and professional.
- Respect different opinions and perspectives.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Help others learn and grow.
- Take responsibility for your actions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, abusive, or inappropriate language.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Responsibilities

Community maintainers may review, edit, or remove contributions that violate this code. They may take appropriate action to maintain a safe and respectful environment.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and community events.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, contact the project maintainer through the repository's GitHub profile or another designated reporting channel.

Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity of the violation, maintainers may:

1. Issue a warning.
2. Temporarily restrict participation.
3. Permanently remove access to the community.
